Log from Phone & WhatsApp

Never be tied to your laptop to track your daily food

1. Install as an App on iPhone or Android (PWA)

NutriTrack is a certified Progressive Web App. When deployed, it installs as a real app icon on your phone with zero app-store hassle:

  • iPhone (Safari): Open your app URL, tap the Share button, then tap "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android (Chrome): Open your app URL, tap the three dots (), then tap "Install App".

2. The Zero-Friction WhatsApp Workflow

WhatsApp has a native "Message Yourself" chat at the top of your chat list. You can jot down foods as you eat throughout the day:

Breakfast: 3 eggs, 2 toast, 1 black coffee
Lunch: 150g grilled chicken, 1 cup white rice, broccoli
Snack: 1 scoop whey, 5g creatine, 1 banana

When you open NutriTrack on your phone, simply tap Chat Log ➔ Paste! Our parser automatically cleans WhatsApp timestamps and logs everything in seconds.

3. Free 24/7 Cloud Hosting (Laptop Can Be OFF)

To access your app from your phone anywhere in the world (even when your laptop is closed and asleep):

• Vercel / Netlify: Connect your folder or GitHub repo for a free https://my-macro-tracker.vercel.app URL.

• GitHub Pages: Free public hosting directly from any GitHub repository.
